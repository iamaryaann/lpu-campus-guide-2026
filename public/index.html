Frequently asked questions

Before choosing
a meal plan.

Key details to review before locking in your hostel dining preference, plus answered student doubts.

Is the hostel mess strictly vegetarian?

Yes. All dining halls inside LPU hostels and campus food courts serve strictly vegetarian food. A non-vegetarian mess is listed adjacent to campus through an independent vendor.

Is taking a university mess plan compulsory?

LPU states that students may opt for a university mess plan or manage meals independently via campus food courts and kiosks. Check any scholarship or specific residential terms before booking.

Where can I check the live daily menu?

Once enrolled, students can view their assigned mess’s daily menu and log meal feedback directly in the LPU Touch mobile app or on the hostel dining notice board.

Is the a la carte or South Indian mess in every hostel?

No. A la carte counters and specialized regional messes (South Indian, Rajasthani, Eastern) operate at selected residential locations and require minimum student enrolment.

How long does one session mess fee cover?

A listed session plan covers one regular academic year (approximately 10 months across two semesters). Winter/summer vacation stay or mid-term upgrades follow university pro-rata rules.

Can I upgrade from 2 or 3 meals to 4 meals later?

Upgrades to a higher meal plan (for example adding evening snacks or moving to 4 meals) are generally permitted via UMS by paying the differential fee, whereas downgrades are restricted once opted.
